A QUICK FIELD GUIDE

What are you looking at?

The 2D Fourier Transform rewrites an image as a collection of repeating patterns, or spatial frequencies. Slow changes in brightness sit at low frequencies; fine edges and texture show up at high frequencies. This explorer computes the transform with a radix-2 FFT and moves zero frequency to the center.

Magnitude shows how much of each frequency is present. The center is the broad shapes; farther from it are finer details. Bright points indicate strong patterns.

Phase records where each wave lines up. It carries important structural information—without phase, the image cannot be reconstructed, even if its magnitude is preserved.
